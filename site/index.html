The studio

Welcome, arrangement, and mixer

On this computer

Smart Mix does the first pass.

Gain, balance, vocal ducking, and a loudness target, as one undo step. It stays on your machine. You can still move every fader after.

A room code

Play it with someone else.

One person hosts, the other joins with the code. Voice and screen share are there. Having the app does not put anyone in your session.

Off until you turn it on

Bring your own model.

The assistant stays off until you add your own key. Studio 4000 does not charge for that key, and it does not keep it.

When the song is ready

Export the mix, or the stems.

A stereo file through the master, or one file per track. Pick the loudness and leave. Then take it to AKAsheet if you want it in front of people.

EffectsBuilt-in processors, a parametric EQ, Airwindows, and your own VST3 plug-ins. Drag the chain, open a few at once.
InstrumentsA piano roll and the computer keyboard. Loop recording, takes, and comping are on the audio side.
It updates itselfOnline, the app checks the public project when it opens and replaces itself when a newer build is published.
